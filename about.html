About Find Bono

Welcome to Find Bono! This game is inspired by classic image search puzzles like Where's Waldo but with a unique twist.

All images are generated with AI, allowing us to create a huge variety of hidden object puzzles. You’ll never run out of challenges, because there’s always something new to find.

Come back every day to test your observation skills with a fresh puzzle. The game is completely free to play online, no downloads, just start playing!

Your mission is simple but tricky: find Bono, the pink elephant. He’s sneaky and hiding somewhere in every puzzle. Good luck! Can you spot Bono?

This is my first ever online project so I hope you enjoy it!
